# Reusable camera experiment record

Export a dated copy before reusing this page for another experiment. Keep the image and mask filenames with the note. Measurements below are blank so the evidence comes from your robot rather than invented results.

:::note experiment_id | Experiment ID, date, people, code version, environment and camera orientation. | 3
:::note question | One question and your prediction before running. State a measurable outcome. | 4
:::note setup | Diagram the setup in words or on paper. Record ruler distance, target size/color, lighting, background and frame size. | 6
:::note variables | Changed variable; fixed variables; repeats; independent ground truth. What might accidentally change? | 5
:::note procedure | Exact commands and trial filenames. Describe enough detail for someone else to repeat the test. | 6
:::note results | Trial-by-trial readings: ROI, matched count, area, ratio, mean value, bbox width and predicted status. Include image/mask/report paths. | 10
:::note diagnosis | Prediction versus observation. Locate the failed stage: acquisition, preprocessing, feature, label, model, timing or policy. Consider at least two explanations. | 7
:::note evidence | What does this result establish? What does it NOT establish? Which conclusion would overclaim? | 5
:::note next | Smallest next test, proposed code change, expected output, and how you will verify it. | 5
:::note ai_log | AI prompt, suggestion, accepted/rejected edits, explanation of new code and test results. | 5
:::check record_complete | I preserved original evidence, recorded failures and exported this experiment's notes.
